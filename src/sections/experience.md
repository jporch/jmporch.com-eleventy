## Application Developer, Harvard Law School, Cambridge, MA

*2016–Present*

At Harvard Law School I serve as part of the Business Solutions team within the Internet Technology Services department. My role supports the planning, development, maintenance, and ongoing support of a stable of internal applications supporting major elements of the school's day-to-day operations. Here I work alongside other departments to ensure the systems provided support their business needs as part of the greater HLS community.

Specific responsibilities include ongoing support and enhancement of the school's [primary public website](https://hls.harvard.edu/) (WordPress/PHP), and [Faculty Bibliography](https://hls.harvard.edu/), an initiative to provide an indexed and searchable interface for accessing HLS faculty scholarship (PHP/JSON/Elasticsearch). I also support Agresso, an internal student information system and custom API layer (C#), and HELIOS, an internal portal that provides staff and faculty access to campus resources (C#/ASP.NET).

## Research Fellow, Trinity Fellows Academy, Royal Oak, MD

*2015–2016*

As a Research Fellow at [Trinity Fellows Academy](https://trinityfellowsacademy.org/), I pursued self-guided research under the guidance of Dr. Syman Stevens and Dr. Laura Cook-Kenna. My focus was the anthropological role of play in human society and its applications in the development of videogames.

I produced a written thesis and supporting essays published at the online videogame criticism journal [First Person Scholar](https://www.firstpersonscholar.com/) as well as the games and culture webzine [Haywire Magazine](https://www.haywiremag.com/). Along with my own research, I served in a writing group with two other Fellows, providing significant feedback, brainstorming, and proofreading for their research theses.

On a weekly basis I served as support staff for the [Osprey Point Retreat and Convention Center](https://www.ospreypoint.org/), learning to develop my hospitality and servant leadership skills under the general manager. This work is a fully integrated part of TFA's vision for developing the character and attitude needed to be a great leader in any field. I also helped provide hospitality and service to lecturers and guests, acted as logistics coordinator for three major weekend conferences, and oversaw work shift scheduling for the year.

## Application Developer, InterSystems Corporation, Cambridge, MA

*2011–2015*

At InterSystems I worked under Benjamin Spead on the Application Services team, using ISC technology to develop and maintain tools for use within the company. Much of my day-to-day effort was dedicated to supporting multiple mission-critical applications. My tasks ranged from triage and categorization of newly reported bugs to meeting with end users to develop specifications for requested features and enhancements.

Projects included InterSystems' change control and workflow management application, a facilities management ticketing and issue tracker, a Customer Relations Management application, and a contractor directory and workflow manager.

I worked extensively with InterSystems technologies such as Caché, Ensemble, ObjectScript, Zen, and Caché Server Pages (CSP), as well as standard web development technologies such as HTML, JavaScript, and CSS for client-side functionality and DOM manipulation.

InterSystems' Change Control Record, a workflow and version control management application I helped develop, was named the best use of Perforce Helix in Medical Technology for its use with [InterSystems TrakCare](https://www.intersystems.com/) at the [2016 Versionary Awards](https://www.perforce.com/), presented during the Perforce MERGE conference.

## Sweezy Research Fellow, Grove City College, Grove City, PA

*2009*

As part of Grove City College's Sweezy Fellowship Research Experience for Undergraduates program, I worked with a team of three other students alongside Dr. Christiaan Gribble pursuing research in ray tracing. The project involved significant experience coding in a conventional UNIX environment and for specialized DSP hardware (SPI's Storm-I).

Our efforts resulted in a fully functional ray tracing engine to facilitate research in fixed- and floating-point environments, a peer-reviewed paper published in the *Journal of Graphics, GPU, and Game Tools*, and a poster at ACM SIGGRAPH/Eurographics High Performance Graphics.

My contributions included fixed-point mathematics libraries for the DSP, design and implementation of the core ray-tracing engine, and writing and editing the JGT article. See [Portfolio](#portfolio).
