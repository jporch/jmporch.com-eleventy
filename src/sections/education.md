## Trinity Fellows Academy, Royal Oak, MD

*2015–2016*

Weekly rotation of guest lecturers speaking on topics such as psychology, theology, political philosophy, social justice, and environmental stewardship. The curriculum was coordinated by Dr. Syman Stevens and included respected speakers such as Dr. Os Guinness, Andy and Catherine Crouch, and Curt Thompson.

Each fellow was responsible for a year-long independent research project and a written thesis with multiple supporting essays. Parts of my writing have been published at [First Person Scholar](https://www.firstpersonscholar.com/) (see [Portfolio](#portfolio)).

## Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Grove City College, Grove City, PA

*2007–2011*

ABET-accredited Computer Science department headed by Dr. William Birmingham. Advised by Dr. Christiaan Gribble. In addition to required classes, I pursued additional mathematics courses in linear algebra and statistics theory, and summer research in mathematics and computer graphics under Dr. Gribble, published in the *Journal of Graphics, GPU, and Game Tools*.

In addition to computer science and mathematics, I studied electives such as Technical Management, Creative Writing, and Fantasy Literature.
